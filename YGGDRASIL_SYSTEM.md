# 🌳 The Yggdrasil Progression System

**QuestLog's core progression mechanic — climb the World Tree from Helheim to Asgard.**

---

## The Concept

Yggdrasil, the Norse World Tree, has **9 realms** stacked vertically. You start at the roots (Helheim, realm of the dead) and climb to the crown (Asgard, hall of the gods).

**Every level you gain moves you up the tree.**  
**Every habit you complete fuels the climb.**

This creates:
- **Clear visual progression** (you literally see yourself moving up the tree)
- **Narrative weight** (you're escaping death, ascending to godhood)
- **Replayability** (prestige = climb again with bonuses)

---

## The Nine Realms (Bottom to Top)

### TIER 1: THE ROOTS (Levels 1-5)
**Theme:** Death, failure, frozen potential

1. **Helheim** (Levels 1-3) — Realm of the Dead  
   *"You awaken in the mists. You fell before. This is your second chance."*  
   **Monsters:** Draugr (undead), Hel-Hounds, Restless Spirits

2. **Niflheim** (Levels 4-5) — Realm of Mist & Ice  
   *"The frost bites deep. Many freeze here, mid-climb. Do not stop moving."*  
   **Monsters:** Ice Wraiths, Frost Serpents, Frozen Husks

---

### TIER 2: THE TRUNK (Levels 6-17)
**Theme:** Mortal struggle, craftsmanship, raw power

3. **Svartalfheim** (Levels 6-9) — Realm of the Dwarves  
   *"The dwarves forge daily. They test your discipline."*  
   **Monsters:** Dark Elves, Iron Golems, Corrupted Smiths

4. **Midgard** (Levels 10-13) — Realm of Mortals  
   *"You have returned to where you fell. Will you fall again?"*  
   **Monsters:** Bandits, Beasts, Corrupted Warriors

5. **Jotunheim** (Levels 14-17) — Realm of Giants  
   *"The giants respect only strength. Prove yours."*  
   **Monsters:** Frost Giants, Stone Behemoths, Giant Wolves

---

### TIER 3: THE CANOPY (Levels 18-32)
**Theme:** Perfection, connection, rebirth through fire

6. **Alfheim** (Levels 18-22) — Realm of the Light Elves  
   *"The light elves demand perfection. Many turn back here, blinded."*  
   **Monsters:** Radiant Sentinels, Perfectionist Constructs, Fallen Seraphim

7. **Vanaheim** (Levels 23-27) — Realm of the Vanir  
   *"The Vanir test your word. Have you kept your promises?"*  
   **Monsters:** Corrupted Druids, Wild Hunt Beasts, Oath-Breakers

8. **Muspelheim** (Levels 28-32) — Realm of Fire  
   *"This is the forge where gods are tempered. Can you endure the heat?"*  
   **Monsters:** Fire Giants, Flame Wraiths, Surtr's Spawn

---

### TIER 4: THE CROWN (Levels 33-40)

9. **Asgard** (Levels 33-40) — Realm of the Gods  
   *"Valhalla awaits. But first, face the final trial."*  
   **Final Boss:** The Fallen Allfather (Odin corrupted by Chaos)  
   **Reward:** Prestige unlock

---

## Prestige: The Eternal Climb

### What Happens When You Reach Asgard?
You defeat the final boss → **Ascend to Valhalla** → Become an Einherjar (honored warrior).

But the Chaos is eternal. The tree needs you again.

**You are reborn at Helheim to climb once more.**

### Prestige Bonuses (Per Ascension)
1. **+5% XP gain** (stacks, max 10 prestiges = +50%)
2. **+5 Skill Points** (unlock more abilities)
3. **Keep 1 Legendary item** (choose which one to carry into next life)
4. **Unlock harder difficulty tiers** (Heroic, Mythic, Legendary modes)
5. **Cosmetic rewards** (golden aura, different sprite, titles)
6. **Prestige-only gear** (e.g., "Gungnir Fragment" — Odin's spear, only drops on Prestige 3+)

### Infinite Prestige
- No cap (climb as many times as you want)
- After Prestige 10, XP bonus caps (but cosmetics/titles/lore keep coming)
- Leaderboard tracks highest prestige count

## Skill Trees: The Branches of Yggdrasil

**Yggdrasil has many branches** → Each is a different skill tree path.

### Four Main Branches (Unlocked from Start)

#### 🔴 The Iron Branch (Health → Strength)
Physical power, endurance, raw damage  
**Skills:** Unbreakable Oath (+10% HP), Battle Fury (+15% damage when low HP), Titan's Grip (dual-wield)

#### 🔵 The Crystal Branch (Mind → Intelligence)
Wisdom, magic, XP efficiency  
**Skills:** Rune Knowledge (+10% XP), Sight Beyond Sight (inspect enemies), Seidr Mastery (magic attacks)

#### 🟡 The Golden Branch (Wealth → Luck)
Fortune, loot drops, treasure hunting  
**Skills:** Midas Touch (+20% gold), Gambler's Favor (+10% rare drops), Dragon's Hoard (hidden chests)

#### ⚪ The Silver Branch (Social → Charisma)
Bonds, leadership, guild synergy  
**Skills:** Oath of Kinship (guild XP bonus), Inspiring Presence (guild stat boost), Valhalla's Call (summon allies)

---

### Prestige-Locked Hybrid Branches

After **Prestige 1**, unlock advanced paths:

#### 🟣 The Twilight Branch (Strength + Intelligence)
**Spellblade builds** — Melee + magic hybrid  
**Skills:** Spellblade (attacks deal magic damage), Runic Strikes (stun chance)

#### 🟠 The Ember Branch (Strength + Luck)
**Critical hit builds** — High damage, loot focus  
**Skills:** Critical Edge (+10% crit), Blood & Gold (crits drop extra gold)

#### 🩵 The Frostbound Branch (Intelligence + Luck)
**Ice mage builds** — Freeze enemies, guaranteed loot  
**Skills:** Ice Shard Mastery (freeze chance), Fortune's Frost (frozen = guaranteed loot)

## Lore Integration (Dark Souls Style)

### How Lore is Revealed
1. **Gear flavor text** — Every item has 2-3 sentences of cryptic lore
2. **Battle log fragments** — 10% chance after defeating certain enemies
3. **Realm discovery** — Unlocking a new realm shows intro lore
4. **Habit completion drops** — 5% chance to get a lore snippet instead of just XP
5. **Hidden lore** — Easter eggs for equipping specific gear combos

### Example Lore Snippets

**Gear (Gungnir's Echo):**
> *"A spear forged in imitation of Odin's legendary weapon. The original was said to never miss its mark. This one... sometimes does. But those who wield it with discipline may one day be worthy of the real thing."*

**Battle Fragment (Fallen Keeper):**
> *"The Dark Knight spoke before fading: 'I kept my oath for 1,000 days. Then I missed one. Just one.' His voice cracked. 'It was enough.'"*

**Realm Discovery (Muspelheim):**
> *"Fire roars in Muspelheim. This is the forge where gods are tempered. The heat is unbearable. Only those who endure daily trials can withstand it. Are you ready to burn?"*

## Visual Design (Pixel Art)

### Yggdrasil Map Screen
- **Vertical scrolling tree** (portrait orientation)
- **9 realm nodes** (64x64 sprites each)
- **Current realm highlighted** (pulsing glow)
- **Locked realms grayed out** (shadowed, fog effect)
- **Branches extend** from trunk (skill tree access points)

### Realm Icons
- Helheim: Skull
- Niflheim: Snowflake
- Svartalfheim: Hammer & Anvil
- Midgard: Sword & Shield
- Jotunheim: Giant Footprint
- Alfheim: Sun/Light Rays
- Vanaheim: Tree/Leaf
- Muspelheim: Flame
- Asgard: Crown/Hall

### Color Gradient
- Roots → Dark (near-black blue)
- Trunk → Mid (slate gray-blue)
- Canopy → Bright (greens, yellows)
- Crown → Gold (Asgard glows)

## Player Journey Example

### First Climb (Prestige 0)
1. Start in **Helheim** (Level 1)
2. Complete "Go to gym" habit → Earn 40 XP
3. Character gains Strength → Can defeat Draugr
4. Battle Draugr → Victory → Loot: Rusty Dagger (Common)
5. Read lore: *"A blade forgotten in the mists..."*
6. Level up to 4 → Realm progresses to **Niflheim**
7. Unlock skill point → Spend on "Unbreakable Oath" (+10% HP)
8. Continue climbing... eventually reach **Asgard** (Level 35)
9. Defeat Fallen Allfather → **Prestige!**

### Second Climb (Prestige 1)
1. Restart at **Helheim** (Level 1, but with +5% XP bonus)
2. Keep 1 Legendary item from previous climb (e.g., Gungnir's Echo)
3. Unlock **Twilight Branch** (Spellblade build)
4. Climb faster with bonuses
5. Prestige again → Repeat

## Why This Works

### Clear Progression
Players can **see** their progress (literally climbing a tree). No abstract "Level 47" — you're in Jotunheim, fighting giants.

### Emotional Weight
The lore reinforces the habit-building message: *You fell before. You're climbing again. Don't stop this time.*

### Replayability
Prestige = new builds, harder difficulty, exclusive loot. The tree is infinite.

### Visual Identity
Yggdrasil is **instantly recognizable**. Norse aesthetic is underused in mobile games. Pixel art makes it charming, not generic.
